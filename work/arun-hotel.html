HospitalityFive Lights Circle, Mysore

Arun Hotel

on a three-sided plot

A hotel, restaurant and banquet hall on a corner plot at Five Lights Circle in Mysore. Rooms line all three edges, drives and parking wrap the outside, and at the centre a triangular atrium holds a circular stair.

In the 3D view, the room wings step down towards the street in planted terraces, and the entrance sits under a deep canopy beside a glazed atrium with a round window.

Plans

Three sides, one heart

The plot is a triangle, so the building is one too. Its edges hold the rooms and halls; its centre holds the atrium and the stair that tie the floors together.

01 / 03

Site plan

Five Lights Circle · Not to scale

  1. The plot sits at Five Lights Circle, between College of Pharmacy Road and Mission Hospital Road, with an existing road along its third side.
  2. Gate 1, the main entry, is at the Five Lights Circle end, and Gate 2, the main exit, at the far end of Mission Hospital Road. Gate 3, at the corner opposite, is the service entry and exit.
  3. Drives with parking bays run along all three sides of the building.
  4. Two lawns line Mission Hospital Road on either side of the entrance forecourt, and planting runs along the compound walls.

02 / 03

Ground floor

Arrival, dining and banquets · Not to scale

  1. The entrance lobby opens off Mission Hospital Road, with feature landscaping and two fountains inside.
  2. Reception and two waiting lounges sit between the lobby and the restaurant. The restaurant takes the narrow end of the plan, with two kitchens, a utility room and a store.
  3. The banquet hall has its own stage and two green rooms, and a separate banquet dining hall runs along the long side.
  4. A circular stair rises at the centre. An outdoor featured area and the ramp down to the basement close the plan at the Gate 2 end.

03 / 03

Typical room floor

Rooms, suites and the atrium · Not to scale

  1. Rooms and suites line all three sides, with balconies on the outer walls.
  2. Along College of Pharmacy Road the rooms step back one by one, following the line of the plot.
  3. Corridors meet at a triangular atrium around the circular stair, with two passenger lifts beside it.
  4. A conference room sits at the far corner.

Construction

How it is put together

  • Basement

    A ramp at the Gate 2 end of the building leads down to the basement.

  • Stair and lifts

    The circular stair stacks at the centre of every floor, with two passenger lifts beside it on the room floors.

  • Facade

    Stone-clad piers and floor bands frame deep balconies above. Timber-toned fins and tall glazing face the street below.

  • Terraces

    The room wings step down towards the street, and each step is a planted terrace.

  • Arrival

    A deep canopy covers the drop-off at the main entrance, behind stone gate piers and the hotel sign.

  • Landscape

    Lawns along Mission Hospital Road, planting along the compound walls and fountains in the entrance lobby.
