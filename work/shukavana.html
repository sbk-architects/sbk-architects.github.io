Shukavana bird museum

a forest of parrots

A bird museum, crafted piece by piece. Under a timber ceiling, bird figures stand along a winding plinth between columns wrapped in bamboo, with the trees outside seen through walls of glass.

At the entrance, painted parrots line the path to a timber sign that carries the name in Kannada, Malayalam, Devanagari and Roman letters.

3D views

Inside the gallery

A single room of glass and timber, where the birds stand along a plinth that winds between the columns, and a flock hangs overhead.

Craft

Crafted piece by piece

  • Bamboo columns

    Four columns are wrapped in bamboo that swells and narrows like a woven basket, with a thin spiral wound up each one for creepers to climb.

  • The plinth

    A low plinth winds around the room in waves. Its timber face is lit from beneath, and the bird figures stand along its top.

  • The hanging flock

    A ring of light hangs from the ceiling, with a flock of small birds suspended from it on fine wires.

  • Lattice screens

    Dark lattice screens stand between the panes, so the trees outside are seen through a pattern of light and shade.

  • The entrance

    Painted parrots on painted boxes line the path to the door, between bonsai on stone plinths, lanterns and vine-covered walls.

  • Roof and glass

    A dark hipped roof and a timber ceiling shelter walls of glass that open the gallery to the trees.
