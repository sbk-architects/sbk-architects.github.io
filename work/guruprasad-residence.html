Guruprasad Residence

stone, timber and tile

A two-storey family home under a sloping tiled roof. Plastered walls are set off by dark stone and timber boards, and a terrace with a wrought-iron railing sits over the car porch.

Inside, the ground floor gathers the living room, an open kitchen and dining, a puja room and a bedroom around the stair; the first floor has a family lounge and two more bedrooms.

Programme
Three bedrooms, living, kitchen and dining, puja room, family lounge
Type
Private home
Floors
Ground and first
Parking
A car porch at the front
Materials

Plans

Two floors around a stair

The stair stands at the middle of the house. Downstairs, the living room faces the road and the kitchen and dining open out at the back; upstairs, the bedrooms open off a family lounge.

01 / 02

Ground floor

Living, kitchen, puja and bedroom 1 · Not to scale

  1. Steps rise from the road to the entrance, beside a car porch.
  2. The foyer leads to the living room at the front, which looks out over a planted strip along the road.
  3. The kitchen, with an island counter, and the dining table share an open room at the back, with a puja room beside them.
  4. A bedroom at the back, a wash counter and two toilets sit beside the stair, and a narrow garden runs along the side of the house.

02 / 02

First floor

Family lounge and two bedrooms · Not to scale

  1. The stair arrives at a family lounge, with a sofa by the window.
  2. The bedroom at the back has a long wardrobe along one wall.
  3. The front bedroom has its own bathroom with a shower, and a dressing counter.
  4. A terrace above the car porch looks onto the street; at the back, a bathroom opens off the landing.

Construction

How it is put together

  • Tiled roof

    A sloping roof of grey tiles covers the front, with a gutter and downpipe along its edge.

  • Tower

    A plastered tower with a thin, projecting cap rises above the roof.

  • Stone and timber

    Dark stone with slim vertical fins, and a panel of vertical timber boards, set off the plastered walls.

  • Balcony

    The terrace over the car porch is edged with a black wrought-iron railing.

  • Windows

    Timber-framed windows, one of them set in a deep plastered frame that stands out from the stone.

  • Porch and gate

    A car porch under the terrace, beside a slatted gate and a planter wall. Wall lights wash the stone and timber at dusk.
