Apollo Hospital

a front block for the hospital

A two-storey block on the hospital's street front: a dining hall and its kitchen below, and above them a doctors' cafe with an outdoor terrace and an HR office. A covered bridge brings doctors across from the hospital.

In the 3D views, white columns and arched windows face the street, a brick arch marks the entrance, and planters line the terrace above.

Ground floor

Dining hall and kitchen · Not to scale

  1. The main entrance opens off the footpath on the road.
  2. The dining hall has its own entry from the side lane, with planting along its length.
  3. The kitchen, with its equipment and a washing area, has a service entry from the lane and its own stair.
  4. A second stair at the front rises to the first floor.

02 / 02

First floor

Doctors' cafe, terrace and HR office · Not to scale

  1. Doctors come across from the hospital on a connecting bridge.
  2. The doctors' cafe seats 10 inside and 18 outdoors, behind bamboo partitions.
  3. An outdoor seating area gathers around a round planter.
  4. The HR office has a reception, a waiting lounge, a manager's cabin, a meeting room and cabins, with washrooms between it and the cafe.

Construction

How it is put together

  • Arcade

    White columns frame a row of arched windows along the street, with a brick-lined arch at the entrance.

  • Cafe pavilion

    Upstairs, the doctors' cafe sits in a glass pavilion under a deep roof lined with timber.

  • Bridge

    A covered bridge links the hospital to the first floor.

  • Kitchen

    The kitchen has its own service entry, washing area and stair, apart from the dining hall's entrance.

  • Planting

    Planters line the terrace edge, bamboo partitions divide the cafe, and planting runs along the side lane.

  • Office

    The HR office takes the far end of the first floor, reached by its own stair from the street.
