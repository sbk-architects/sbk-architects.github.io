Mahadev’s Residence

lit at dusk

A family home built up in boxes of ribbed grey cladding. Creepers spill over the parapets of the top floor under a thin cantilevered roof, and a frame lined in timber pushes out around the first-floor window.

At street level, white fins and bamboo screen the porch, and a wall of stone panels and white jaali blocks runs along the pavement.

3D views

By day and at dusk

From the street, the top floor reads as two planted boxes either side of a white recess with louvres. Below them, a balcony screened by terracotta jaali sits beside the timber frame, and a lower wing with timber shutters steps down to the boundary wall.

Construction

How it is put together

  • Cladding

    Boxes of vertical ribbed panels in warm grey wrap the upper floors.

  • Roof and planting

    A thin roof slab cantilevers over the top floor, and creepers spill over the parapets below it.

  • Timber frame

    A deep frame lined in timber pushes out around the first-floor window, with slatted shutters behind it.

  • Jaali

    Terracotta jaali screens the balcony, and white jaali blocks make the boundary wall.

  • Light

    Wall lights wash the cladding at dusk, and a band of light runs under each floor.

  • Street front

    Parking behind a slatted gate, white fins and bamboo along the porch, and the entrance under a canopy hung on a tie rod.
