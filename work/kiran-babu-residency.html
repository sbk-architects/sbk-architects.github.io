Kiran Babu’s Residency

a roof of light

A family home under a thin, floating roof. White piers hold up a soffit of timber slats set with downlights, and terracotta jaali screens the open terrace beneath it.

Below, the first floor is wrapped in timber, with a balcony behind a planter of creepers and a bay of vertical timber fins.

Character

Type
Private home

3D views

By night and by day

From the front, the roof frame spans the whole house. Jaali parapets edge the terrace, the timber-clad first floor opens onto the balcony, and the gates and a timber wall with planting line the street.

Construction

How it is put together

  • Roof

    A thin flat roof, its soffit lined with timber slats and downlights, spans the open terrace on white piers.

  • Jaali

    Terracotta jaali screens the terrace and a strip up the side, letting light and air through.

  • Timber

    The first floor is clad in timber, with sliding glass doors onto the balcony.

  • Fins

    A bay of vertical timber fins screens the windows on the right.

  • Planting

    A concrete planter wraps the balcony, with creepers spilling over its edge and a frangipani growing in it.

  • Street front

    Black slatted gates under a stone-clad frame lead to parking for two cars, beside a timber wall with broad-leaved planting.
