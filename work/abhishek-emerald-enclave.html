ResidentialPrivate home

Abhishek Emerald Enclave

inside and out

A four-bedroom home on three levels, set in a planted garden. Courts, timber decks and a stair around a court open to the sky bring light and green into every floor, and the terrace is given over to dining and sitting outdoors.

In the 3D view, grey patterned tiles clad the walls, deep slabs shade the windows and creepers spill over from the roof garden.

Programme
Four bedrooms, living, kitchen and dining, library, lift
Type
Private home
Floors
Ground, first and terrace
Outdoors
Courts, teak decks, terrace, garden with an outhouse

Plans

Courts, decks and a garden

Every floor keeps a piece of the garden: a court, a deck or a seat in the open. At the centre, a court open to the sky rises through the stair from the ground floor to the terrace.

01 / 04

Master plan

House and garden · Not to scale

  1. The house stands back from the road on one side of the plot, leaving a large lawn beside it.
  2. From the road, an entry path and a flight of steps run up along the side of the house.
  3. A teak wood deck wraps the living room and steps out to the lawn.
  4. A pathway links the house to an outhouse at the back of the garden, and dense planting lines every boundary.

02 / 04

Ground floor

Arrival, living and bedroom 1 · Not to scale

  1. Steps from the road rise to the entrance, beside an outdoor gazebo.
  2. The foyer (11'6" × 8'0") opens to the living room (16'0" × 20'0") and to the kitchen and dining (13'2" × 25'10").
  3. A court on one side, and a planted court open to the sky inside the stair, bring daylight into the middle of the plan.
  4. Bedroom 1 has a walk-in wardrobe and its own toilet, and a semi-outdoor deck extends the living room towards the garden.

03 / 04

First floor

Bedrooms 2 and 3, library · Not to scale

  1. Bedrooms 2 and 3 each have their own toilet; bedroom 2 opens onto a 4 ft wide balcony.
  2. Bedroom 3 looks onto an inner court with its own balcony.
  3. A library unit sits at the top of the stair, beside the lift.
  4. Semi-outdoor seating opens off the landing.

04 / 04

Terrace floor

Outdoor rooms and bedroom 4 · Not to scale

  1. An outdoor dining space with a bar counter.
  2. Formal and informal outdoor seating, the informal corner edged with planting.
  3. Bedroom 4, with its own toilet and a 4 ft wide balcony.
  4. The stair and its court open to the sky continue up to the terrace.

Construction

How it is put together

  • Stair and lift

    The stair wraps a court open to the sky on every floor, and a lift serves the ground and first floors.

  • Courts

    Courts on the ground and first floors, and planted pockets open to the sky, bring daylight into the middle of the house.

  • Decks

    Timber decks run along the garden side, with a semi-outdoor deck beside the living room.

  • Facade

    In the 3D view, grey patterned tiles clad the walls, and deep slabs shade the windows on each floor.

  • Terrace

    The terrace holds an outdoor dining space with a bar counter, formal and informal seating, and a fourth bedroom.

  • Garden

    Lawns and dense planting surround the house, and a pathway leads through the garden to the outhouse.
