Chandrashekar’s Residency

frames of light

A family home that rises in layers above its garages. White frames and deep soffits, each set with downlights, hold panels of textured stone, timber louvres, grey cladding and dark vertical fins.

Inside, a pooja room of carved timber and glass stands beside a stair of floating timber treads.

3D views

The house in layers

On the street front, steps climb beside the garages to the entrance. A stone-clad bay holds two floors of timber-framed windows, a panel of timber louvres runs up the middle, and a slatted pergola shades the roof terrace.

Inside

The pooja room

Beside the stair

  1. Carved timber double doors, with a round ornament at their centre and small inset motifs above and below.
  2. Glass walls let the idol be seen from the stair and the living room.
  3. A timber canopy with downlights hangs over it on slim brass rods.
  4. Beside it, the stair rises on floating timber treads behind a frameless glass balustrade, past a lattice screen.

Construction

How it is put together

  • Frames

    Each floor projects as a crisp white frame, with downlights set into its soffit.

  • Stone

    Textured stone bays hold tall timber-framed windows and slit windows, washed by wall lights.

  • Fins

    Panels of dark vertical fins screen parts of the lower floors.

  • Louvres

    Horizontal timber louvres wrap the top floor.

  • Balconies

    A recessed corner balcony with planting behind a glass railing, and another with seating facing the street.

  • Street front

    Garages behind timber doors, a lamp post, and a lit garden wall with planting along the pavement.
