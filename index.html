01/ 07Drawing

Every building begins as a line.

We walk the plot, read the sun path and listen to how you live. Then we draw it all in-house: plans, elevations, 3D views and the structural design, Vastu-aware if you want it.

Site survey · Concept & 3D views · Plan sanction

02/ 07Foundation

Footings sized for your soil.

A soil test sets the footing sizes before any digging starts. Plinth beams then tie the column grid together, so the house stands on ground engineered for its exact plot.

Soil test · Isolated footings · M25 concrete · Fe500 steel

03/ 07Frame

A frame cast floor by floor.

Columns, beams and slabs go up to drawings our own engineers sign. Every pour is cube-tested, and you get photographs from site every week.

RCC frame · Cube tests at 7 & 28 days · Weekly site reports

04/ 07Masonry

Walls, wiring and water.

Brick walls rise course by course, with conduits and plumbing laid in sequence, so nothing gets chased out of a finished wall later.

Wire-cut bricks · Concealed conduits · Pressure-tested lines

05/ 07Envelope

Closed up before the monsoon.

Glazing, waterproofing, railings and cladding close the shell. Every junction is detailed to keep water out and to age well.

Glazed sliders · Membrane waterproofing · Teak fins

06/ 07Finishes

The parts you touch every day.

Floors, joinery, paint and landscape are finished by craftspeople we have worked with for years. Then the scaffolding comes down.

Kota stone · Lime plaster · Teak joinery · Landscape

07/ 07Handover

Lights on. Welcome home.

One team, one contract and one point of responsibility from the first line to the final light, with a year of aftercare once you move in.

As-built drawings · Warranty file · 12 months of care

The studio

We are architects who build, and builders who draw.

The people who draw your home are the people who build it.

Most homes pass through three or four firms before anyone moves in, and something gets lost at every hand-off. With us the drawings stay true on site, the budget stays in one place, and you always know whom to call.

Design studio
Architects, interior designers and 3D visualisers
Engineering
Structural and MEP engineers who check every drawing
Site team
Site engineers, supervisors and crews we have trained for years

Why design and build together

One team, start to finish

  • One contract, start to finish

    Design, approvals, construction and interiors sit under a single agreement. No hand-offs between firms and no finger-pointing when something needs fixing.

  • Engineered to outlast

    Structural design done in-house, materials tested on site, and details drawn for our weather: harsh sun, dust and a long monsoon.

  • Clear to the last rupee

    An itemised BOQ before we break ground, payments tied to milestones, and a photo report from site every week.
